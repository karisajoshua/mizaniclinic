
# Dashboard Overview

Your ambassador dashboard is the central hub for managing your referrals, tracking earnings, and accessing all platform features.

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Quick Stats
Real-time overview of your performance:

- **Total Earnings**: Your accumulated commissions
- **Active Referrals**: Number of people you've referred
- **This Month**: Current month's performance
- **Rank**: Your position in the ambassador hierarchy

### 2. Earnings Overview
Detailed breakdown of your income streams:

- **Referral Commissions**: Money from direct referrals
- **Service Commissions**: Earnings from service bookings
- **Tier Bonuses**: Additional rewards from tier progression
- **Pending Payouts**: Money waiting to be transferred

### 3. Referral Management
Tools for managing your referral network:

- **Your Referral Code**: Unique code for sharing
- **Recent Referrals**: Latest people who joined with your code
- **Referral Performance**: Success rates and conversion metrics
- **Share Tools**: Quick sharing via WhatsApp, SMS, social media

### 4. Commission Tier Status
Track your progress through ambassador tiers:

- **Current Tier**: Your current commission level
- **Next Milestone**: Requirements for tier advancement
- **Progress Bar**: Visual indicator of tier progression
- **Tier Benefits**: Unlocked features and higher commission rates

### 5. Bonus Progress
Monitor your progress toward major rewards:

- **MPA Points**: Progress toward motorbike (1,000 points)
- **Car Bonus**: Progress toward car reward (6,000 points)
- **Achievement Badges**: Milestones and recognition

### 6. Country Distribution
See where your referrals are located:

- **Regional Breakdown**: Referrals by Tanzanian region
- **Growth Trends**: Expansion patterns over time
- **Performance by Area**: Which regions perform best

## Navigation Menu

### Main Sections
- **Dashboard**: Overview and quick stats
- **Referrals**: Detailed referral management
- **Earnings**: Financial tracking and payout history
- **Appointments**: Healthcare booking system
- **Profile**: Account settings and preferences

### Quick Actions
- **Share Code**: Instantly share your referral code
- **Book Appointment**: Schedule with Dr. Mwaka
- **View Earnings**: Detailed financial breakdown
- **Update Profile**: Modify account information

## Mobile Experience

The dashboard is fully optimized for mobile devices:

- **Responsive Design**: Works on all screen sizes
- **Touch-Friendly**: Easy navigation on smartphones
- **Offline Support**: Basic functionality without internet
- **Push Notifications**: Real-time updates on referrals and earnings

## Customization Options

### Dashboard Widgets
Customize your dashboard layout:

- **Widget Order**: Rearrange sections by importance
- **Visibility**: Hide/show specific widgets
- **Refresh Rate**: Set automatic update frequency

### Notification Preferences
Control what notifications you receive:

- **New Referrals**: Instant alerts for new sign-ups
- **Earnings Updates**: Commission and payout notifications
- **System Messages**: Important platform updates

## Performance Metrics

### Key Indicators
Track these important metrics:

- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of shared codes that result in registrations
- **Average Earnings**: Monthly income trends
- **Referral Quality**: Success rate of your referrals
- **Growth Rate**: Network expansion speed

### Benchmarking
Compare your performance:

- **Regional Averages**: How you compare to other ambassadors in your area
- **National Rankings**: Your position among all ambassadors
- **Goal Tracking**: Progress toward personal targets
