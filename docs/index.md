## What is Mizani Clinic Ambassador?

The Mizani Clinic Ambassador platform is a comprehensive referral-based registration and healthcare booking system designed specifically for Tanzania and East Africa. It enables users to:

- **Register with referral codes** and build ambassador networks
- **Earn commissions** through multiple revenue streams
- **Book healthcare appointments** with Dr. Mwaka
- **Track performance** with detailed analytics
- **Manage payments** through mobile money integration
